---
title: Integrity verification
description: Check that a thread's recorded history hasn't been changed.
---

Threadify signs each recorded event and links it to the one before, forming a
chain. If any stored event is changed, removed or reordered, verification fails
at that point.

## Verify a thread

```sh
threadify-cli threads verify --id THREAD_ID
```

The command prints the result and exits with a non-zero status if verification
fails. To verify from code, use the
[integrity queries](/api-reference/integrity-verification).

## What a passing check means

It means the history is exactly what Threadify received. It doesn't mean the run
was correct: an intact chain can contain a failed step or a contract violation.
And it can't prove that something your systems did but never reported didn't
happen. Use [contracts](/core-concepts/creating-contracts) to check behaviour,
and the chain to trust the record.

## Keep your signing secrets

The chain is signed with the hash-chain secret in your Engine config. Keep every
version you've used. If you rotate to a new secret, history signed with the old
one can only be verified while the old secret is still configured. See
[Keep your data](/self-hosting#keep-your-data).

Very recent events may not be included until they've been saved, which usually
takes a few seconds.
