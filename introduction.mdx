Threadify records execution, checks shared rules, and connects history across
services and AI agents. Your application runs the work; Threadify acts as its referee.

| Task | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Record operations and outcomes | SDK steps or OpenTelemetry spans |
| Define order, approvals, and content rules | Gherkin-style contracts |
| Check permission before an action | An explicit SDK `waitFor` call |
| Review history and metrics across runs | Entity profiles |

Keep each contract focused on a section of a workflow, such as refund approval.
References connect related work.

Run an Engine for your company. It serves the dashboard and APIs, stores history
in your PostgreSQL database, and gets license allowances from Fused Registry.

wait for permission before executing it.
