title: Introduction
description: Record how your services and agents execute work, check it against your rules, and see the history in one place.
---

Threadify records each run of a business process as a **thread**: the steps your
services and AI agents performed, what they reported, and whether that matched
the rules you set. You keep running the work; Threadify keeps the record and
checks it.

You can use it in three ways, and most teams start with the first:

- **Record.** Send steps from the SDK, or point your existing OpenTelemetry
  exporter at Threadify.
- **Check.** Write a contract that says which steps may run, in what order, by
  whom, and with what data. Threadify validates every run against it.
- **Gate.** Before a risky action, such as issuing a refund or letting an agent
  call a tool, ask Threadify whether the contract allows it, and only continue
  if it does.

Threadify runs as an **Engine** that you host. It serves the dashboard and the
APIs, and stores history in your PostgreSQL database.
